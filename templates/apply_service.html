Application Assistance Service

Not sure how to complete your university application, or don't have time to do it yourself? I'll complete and submit your online application for you.

Service Fee: R40

Payable only once your application has been fully completed and submitted.

Note: this R40 covers my service only. The university's own application fee (if any) is paid by you, directly to the university.

Documents You'll Need to Have Ready

  • Certified copy of your ID document
  • Certified copy of your matric results (or latest Grade 11/12 results if still in matric)
  • Proof of residence (required by some universities)
  • A recent passport-sized photo (required by some universities)
  • Parent/guardian consent form, if you are under 18

Important: not every certifier is accepted by every university - some only accept certification from a police station, others require a university registrar, public notary, or SA embassy specifically. Check your chosen university's exact requirement before getting your documents certified.

How Your Information Is Handled

Your documents are used only to complete your application and are not kept longer than necessary or shared with anyone else.
